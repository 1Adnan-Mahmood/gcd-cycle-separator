02 Detect cycle boundaries

DISCHARGE → CHARGE

Observed potential window will appear after separation. With window limits, a full discharge reaches the lower limit plus E-step tolerance, after reaching the upper limit minus tolerance. Overshoots are allowed. Blank E step uses 1% of the specified window when both limits are entered. Values are never clipped or changed.

A boundary is confirmed after a voltage fall and rise exceed the threshold. Smaller fluctuations are ignored. Lower the threshold for missed cycles; raise it if noise creates extra cycles. Original potential values are preserved.
